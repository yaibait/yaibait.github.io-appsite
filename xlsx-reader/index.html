Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Learn more about how XLSX Reader works and keeps your data secure.

Is XLSX Reader free to use?
Yes! You can view, edit, calculate formulas, and export spreadsheets for free. An optional VIP one-time upgrade is available if you prefer a 100% ad-free experience.
Does the app upload my Excel files to external servers?
No, never. XLSX Reader operates entirely on your local device. Your spreadsheets, numbers, and documents are never transmitted or stored on any cloud server.
Can I export my sheets to PDF or print them directly?
Absolutely. You can convert any worksheet to a high-quality PDF document with one tap, or print it directly using Apple AirPrint / wireless printing.
What spreadsheet formats are supported?
XLSX Reader supports Microsoft Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and Comma-Separated Values (.csv) formats seamlessly.
