Privacy Policy

Your photos and creative vision belong exclusively to you.

1. Overview & Core Privacy Guarantee

At PhotoEye, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Our app is architected from the ground up so that your photos, camera frames, and aesthetic analyses never leave your device.

2. On-Device AI Processing

All image processing, including real-time framing guidance, rule-of-thirds composition scoring, saliency detection, and cinematic film simulation (such as color grading and grain generation), is performed 100% locally on your iPhone using Apple Neural Engine & Metal. We do not operate image-processing servers and do not upload your media to the cloud.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Camera Access (NSCameraUsageDescription): Required strictly to display the live viewfinder, compute composition guides, and capture photos at your request.
  • Photo Library Access (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription & NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Required strictly to save your captured pictures and edited masterpieces directly to your local Apple Photos library.

4. Advertising & Anonymous Analytics

In the free tier of PhotoEye, non-personalized or privacy-compliant ads may be served through Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK.

  • We comply with Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites without your permission.
  • When upgrading to PhotoEye Pro, all third-party advertising SDK calls are permanently disabled, providing an ad-free and tracking-free darkroom experience.

5. In-App Purchases & Payments

All transactions for PhotoEye Pro are handled securely and directly through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details.

6. Children’s Privacy

PhotoEye does not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us at:
support@photoeye.app
