1. Core Commitment to Children's Privacy

At Math Kingdom 3D (Vương Quốc Toán), child safety and digital privacy take absolute precedence over everything else. Developed by BinhMMO with pure educational intent, our game strictly adheres to:

  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA - United States): Zero collection of personal identifiable information (PII) from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
  • General Data Protection Regulation for Children (GDPR-K - European Union): Strict adherence to data minimization principles.
  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Section 1.3 Kids Category & Section 5.1 Privacy).
  • Google Play Families Policy requirements.

2. Data We Collect and Do NOT Collect

We practice absolute data minimization: our systems retain only the bare minimum technical parameters necessary for the game to function and record high score achievements.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

The minimal parameters transmitted to our backend server (`binhmmo.com`) are utilized strictly for the following legitimate purposes:

  1. Displaying top scores on the Global Leaderboard (API `get_leaderboard.php`).
  2. Recording personal best milestones and returning ranking positions (API `submit_score.php`).
  3. Validating score integrity against mathematical logic to maintain a fair, cheat-free environment for young learners.

We pledge to NEVER sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to data brokers or third parties for commercial marketing or profiling purposes.

4. Third-Party Advertising & COPPA Compliance (Google AdMob)

To keep the game accessible free of charge, the application integrates the Google AdMob mobile ads SDK (via the official Poing Studios plugin).

We implement the strictest child-directed technical safeguards directly in our game source code:

// Game source code configuration in AdMobManager.gd:
req_config.tag_for_child_directed_treatment = RequestConfiguration.TagForChildDirectedTreatment.TRUE
req_config.max_ad_content_rating = RequestConfiguration.MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G
  • Tag for Child-Directed Treatment (COPPA): Google AdMob completely disables interest-based advertising (personalized profiling) and ceases collecting persistent advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS / GAID on Android) for tracking children.
  • Maximum Ad Content Rating (Rating G - General Audiences): Only family-safe advertisements suitable for all age groups are permitted to display.
  • To learn more about Google's data handling practices for children's apps, please visit: Google Advertising Policies.

5. Data Storage & Security Safeguards

We employ industry-standard technical and operational safeguards to protect leaderboard score data:

  • Transport Encryption (HTTPS/TLS): All communication between the game client and our server (`binhmmo.com`) is encrypted using modern TLS/SSL protocols, safeguarding against eavesdropping or man-in-the-middle tampering.
  • Database Protection: Our MySQL backend is isolated behind strict firewall rules, protected against SQL injection attacks, and regularly backed up.

6. Parental Rights & Guardian Controls

Under COPPA and GDPR-K, parents and legal guardians hold full legal authority over their children's data:

  1. Right to Opt Out: Parents may request that their child's nickname and score records be excluded from public leaderboard displays.
  2. Right of Review: Parents may request an overview of any score entries associated with the nickname used by their child.
  3. Right to Erasure (Permanent Deletion): Parents have the unconditional right to demand complete deletion of their child's nickname and score history from our backend databases at any time.

7. Data Retention Policy

Nickname and score entries on the Leaderboard are maintained solely for ongoing in-game motivational rankings. Annually, or upon seasonal competition resets, outdated or inactive score records may be purged from the active database.
