1. Summary & Core Principle

At Utility Desk ("we", "our", or "us"), we firmly believe that your private life, confidential photographs, personal videos, and notes belong solely to you. We have purposely designed Utility Desk with a 100% On-Device Architecture.

Core Principle: All media files, encrypted notes, and measurement data stored inside Utility Desk reside strictly on your local device. We do not operate remote storage servers, and we never transmit, upload, or sell your personal files.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because Utility Desk operates on a decentralized, local-first paradigm:

  • No Cloud Media Storage: We do not store, view, or analyze your imported or captured photos and videos.
  • No Master PIN or Password Retrieval: Your Master PIN, Decoy PIN, and Destroy PIN are hashed and securely stored on your device's hardware keychain (iOS Secure Storage). They are never sent across the internet.
  • No Personal Identification Required: You do not need to register an account, sign up with an email, or link social media profiles to use Utility Desk.
  • No Secret Notes Access: Notes created inside the app are saved locally and are never accessible to our team or any external party.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Utility Desk requests only the minimum iOS permissions necessary to operate its core measurement tools and private vault features. Permissions are only utilized when explicitly initiated by you:

A. Camera Permission (NSCameraUsageDescription)

Used exclusively when you activate the Direct Secret Camera feature. This allows you to snap photos and record videos that save directly into your encrypted vault without creating a copy in your system Camera Roll.

B. Microphone Permission (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription)

Required only to capture audio when recording videos through the Secret Camera feature. Audio data is bundled directly with the video file inside the local vault.

C. Photo Library Access (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription & NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription)

Requested when you choose to import existing photos or videos from your device's Photo Library into Utility Desk, or when you explicitly select a file to export back to your system gallery. We only access the specific items you select.

D. Motion & Accelerometer Sensors

Utilized exclusively by the Bubble Spirit Level tool to calculate pitch, roll, and angle tilt in real time. Sensor data is processed entirely in device memory and is never recorded or logged.

4. Third-Party Services & Subscriptions

To maintain, develop, and distribute Utility Desk, we integrate select trusted third-party SDKs:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

The free version of Utility Desk displays non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device-specific information (such as advertising identifier, IP address, and interaction data) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

You may opt out of personalized tracking via iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) settings, or upgrade to Utility Desk PRO to permanently remove all third-party advertisements and ad networks.

B. RevenueCat & Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases)

We use RevenueCat to validate Apple In-App Purchase receipts and activate PRO entitlements. All billing transactions, credit card handling, and subscriptions are managed securely by Apple App Store. We never have access to your credit card or billing details.

5. Private Browser Sandboxing

The built-in Incognito Web Browser operates within an isolated sandbox environment.

  • Browser cookies, session states, and cached files are stored locally within the application sandbox.
  • Your browsing activity is completely separate from Safari and Google Chrome.
  • You can clear all cookies, history, and cache instantly using the "Clear Cache & Log Out" action.

6. Security & Local Encryption

We employ industry-standard security mechanisms to ensure your personal space remains impenetrable:

  • Local Encryption: Stored files are sandboxed and shielded by iOS data protection.
  • Biometric Security: Biometric authentication (Face ID or Touch ID) utilizes Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Your biometric templates never leave the Apple Secure Enclave.
  • Emergency Decoy & Wipe: The Decoy PIN opens an isolated dummy vault, while the Emergency Destroy PIN irrevocably deletes all private vault contents.

7. Data Retention & Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under European (GDPR), California (CCPA), and global privacy frameworks, you retain full rights to access, modify, and delete your data:

  • Total Erasure: Because your data is stored strictly on your device, deleting the Utility Desk app or executing the Destroy PIN immediately and irreversibly wipes all stored data from existence.
  • Export: You can export your photos and videos back to your device camera roll at any time prior to deletion.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Utility Desk is a general audience utility application and does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through our app, please contact us immediately so we can address it.
