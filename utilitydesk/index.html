Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Utility Desk, daily measurement tools, and vault privacy.

Utility Desk is an all-in-one mobile toolkit combining practical everyday measurement tools (Calculator, Screen Caliper Ruler, Spirit Bubble Level, Angle Protractor, Unit Converter) with an on-device personal vault for safely storing confidential photos, videos, and private notes.

The Calculator functions as a full-featured everyday arithmetic tool. When configured with vault protection, entering your secret PIN and pressing the "=" button opens your private vault. You can also unlock directly with Face ID or Touch ID.

100% locally on your iOS device within the app's secure sandboxed storage. Your files and notes are never uploaded to any cloud server or company database, giving you complete sovereign control over your privacy.

Because Utility Desk does not maintain remote servers or bypass backdoors, only you possess the master PIN. If enabled, you can unlock using Face ID or Touch ID. We strongly encourage keeping your PIN in a safe place.

The Decoy PIN allows you to set up an alternative passcode that unlocks an innocent, dummy vault. The Emergency Destroy PIN immediately and irrevocably erases all encrypted vault contents in critical emergencies.

Subscriptions are managed securely by Apple. You can cancel auto-renewal anytime in your iPhone Settings under Apple ID > Subscriptions. If reinstalling or switching devices, tap "Restore Purchases" in the app's PRO screen.
